CapyGame — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 15, 2026

CapyGame is a standalone 3D universe simulator, world builder, and multiphysics game archipelago designed for players of all ages. Protecting player privacy—especially for younger explorers using Basic Mode—is core to our architecture.

1. Data We Collect

CapyGame does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. We do not integrate third-party advertising networks, behavioral tracking SDKs, cross-app identifiers, or external telemetry servers.

2. On-Device Storage

All game data remains strictly on your device using standard browser storage:

  • Progression and Discovery Stickers: Saved locally under capy_game_save_v3 (unlocks, micro-glade specimen stickers, coins, DNA, and world builds).
  • Settings and Preferences: Saved locally under capy_game_settings_v1 (Basic or Advanced UI mode, camera, audio, and graphics quality).
  • Procedural World & Setup Preferences: Saved locally under capy_game_procedural_config_v1 and capy_game_onboarding_v1 (custom simulation parameters, seed presets, and first-run setup state).
  • Best Parkour Ghost Recording: Saved locally under capy_game_yuzu_ghost_v1 (quantized local Yuzu Mountain time-trial ghost replay).
  • Offline App Cache: Stored locally in Service Worker Cache Storage so CapyGame runs offline.

3. Device Capabilities and Accessibility Audio

When enabled by the user, "Read Aloud" scale cards use the device's built-in Web Speech API (speechSynthesis) directly on-device. CapyGame does not record microphone audio, access camera feeds, request location data, or read contacts.

4. Managing or Deleting Your Data

You retain complete control over local game data. You can export or import save snapshots inside the game settings panel, reset local saves at any time, or clear app storage from your Android or browser settings.

5. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or Google Play Store releases, open an issue on the CapyGame Support & Issues tracker or return to CapyGame.
