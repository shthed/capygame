CapyGame — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 26, 2026

CapyGame is a standalone 3D universe simulator, world builder, and multiphysics game archipelago designed for players of all ages. CapyGame runs with zero backend servers and zero developer-hosted databases.

1. Data We Collect

CapyGame does not operate game servers and does not collect, sell, or share personal data with third parties. We do not integrate advertising networks, behavioral tracking SDKs, cross-app identifiers, or external analytics servers.

2. On-Device Storage

By default, all game data remains strictly on your device using standard browser storage:

  • Progression and Discovery Stickers: Saved locally under capy_game_save_v3 (unlocks, micro-glade specimen stickers, coins, DNA, Capytorio factory snapshots, and Sanctuary builds) plus a local timestamp under capy_game_save_updated_ms.
  • Settings and Preferences: Saved locally under capy_game_settings_v1 (Basic or Advanced UI mode, camera, audio, and graphics quality).
  • Procedural World & Setup Preferences: Saved locally under capy_game_procedural_config_v1 and capy_game_onboarding_v1 (custom simulation parameters, seed presets, and first-run setup state).
  • Best Parkour Ghost Recording: Saved locally under capy_game_yuzu_ghost_v1 (quantized local Yuzu Mountain time-trial ghost replay).
  • Offline App Cache: Stored locally in Service Worker Cache Storage so CapyGame runs offline.

3. Optional Cloud Sync (Google Drive & Play Games)

Cloud sync is strictly optional and disabled until you explicitly choose to sign in inside Settings:

  • Google Drive Sync (Web & Android TWA): When enabled by the player, CapyGame authenticates directly from your browser using Google Sign-In with minimal drive.file permission. CapyGame can only create, read, update, or delete its own CapyGame-Cloud-Save.json backup file inside your personal Google Drive. Access tokens are held strictly in browser memory for the active session, are never written to localStorage or sessionStorage, and are cleared on sign-out.
  • Google Play Games Services (Native Android Builds): When packaged with the optional Android Play Games bridge, players may sign in with their Google Play Games profile to store save snapshots directly in Google Play Games Saved Games.

4. Device Capabilities and Accessibility Audio

When enabled by the user, "Read Aloud" scale cards use the device's built-in Web Speech API (speechSynthesis) directly on-device. CapyGame does not record microphone audio, access camera feeds, request location data, or read contacts.

5. Managing or Deleting Your Data

You retain complete control over your game saves. You can export or import full-profile JSON backups inside Settings (Basic or Advanced Mode), reset local saves at any time, delete your CapyGame-Cloud-Save.json cloud backup from Settings or directly in Google Drive, or clear app storage from your Android or browser settings.

6. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or Google Play Store releases, open an issue on the CapyGame Support & Issues tracker or return to CapyGame.
